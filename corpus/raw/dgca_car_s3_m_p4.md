1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Passengers with confirmed reservations are sometimes denied boarding, or their flights are cancelled or
delayed. This Civil Aviation Requirement (CAR) lays down the minimum facilities and compensation that an airline
shall provide to passengers in such cases. An airline may provide facilities or compensation more favourable to
the passenger than those laid down here.

1.2 This CAR is issued under the provisions of Rule 133A of the Aircraft Rules, 1937.

2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "Alternate flight" means a flight offered by the airline to a passenger, in place of the flight on which the
passenger held a confirmed reservation, to the same destination, which is acceptable to the passenger. A passenger
who travels on the alternate flight is deemed to have accepted it.

2.2 "Block time" means the total time from the moment the aircraft first moves for the purpose of taking off until
the moment it finally comes to rest at the end of the flight, as published in the airline's schedule for the
booked flight.

2.3 "Booked scheduled departure time" means the departure time printed on the passenger's ticket or booking
confirmation for the flight on which the passenger held a confirmed reservation.

2.4 "Basic fare" means the fare component of the ticket excluding taxes, airport charges, user development fees
and any other levies collected on behalf of third parties.

2.5 "Airline fuel charge" means the fuel surcharge levied by the airline and shown separately on the ticket.

2.6 "Cancellation" means the non-operation of a flight which was previously planned and on which at least one
place was reserved.

2.7 "Delay" means the departure of a flight later than the booked scheduled departure time.

2.8 "Denied boarding" means a refusal to carry passengers on a flight, although they have presented themselves for
boarding under the conditions laid down in para 3.1.2, except where there are reasonable grounds to deny them
boarding, such as reasons of health, safety or security, or inadequate travel documentation.

2.9 "Downgrading" means carrying a passenger in a class of travel lower than the class for which the ticket was
purchased.

2.10 "Extraordinary circumstances" means circumstances beyond the control of the airline, as described in para 3.6,
which could not have been avoided even if all reasonable measures had been taken.

2.11 "Facilities" means meals and refreshments, hotel accommodation, and transfer between the airport and the place
of accommodation, provided free of charge to the passenger in accordance with para 3.4.

3. REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Applicability

3.1.1 This CAR shall apply to all flights operated by scheduled Indian air carriers, on domestic sectors and on
international sectors. Flights operated by foreign air carriers are not covered by this CAR.

3.1.2 This CAR shall apply to passengers who have a confirmed reservation on the flight concerned and who have
presented themselves for check-in as stipulated by the airline, and in any case not later than one hour before
the published departure time of the flight.

3.1.3 This CAR shall not apply to passengers travelling free of charge or at a reduced fare not available directly
or indirectly to the public, or to claims relating to baggage, which are governed by the Carriage by Air Act, 1972.

3.2 Denied boarding

3.2.1 When an airline expects to deny boarding on a flight, it shall first call for volunteers to surrender their
reservations in exchange for such benefits as the airline may offer. Passengers who volunteer are not entitled to
compensation under para 3.2.2.

3.2.2 If boarding is denied to passengers against their will, the airline shall pay compensation as follows:
(a) No compensation shall be payable if the airline arranges an alternate flight that is scheduled to depart within
one hour of the booked scheduled departure time of the original reservation.
(b) If the alternate flight is scheduled to depart more than one hour and within 24 hours of the booked scheduled
departure time, compensation equal to 200% of the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, subject to a
maximum of INR 10,000.
(c) If the alternate flight is scheduled to depart more than 24 hours after the booked scheduled departure time,
compensation equal to 400% of the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, subject to a maximum of
INR 20,000.
(d) If the passenger does not opt for the alternate flight, a full refund of the ticket and compensation equal to
400% of the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, subject to a maximum of INR 20,000.

3.2.3 The airline shall not be liable to pay compensation under para 3.2.2 where the passenger did not present
himself for check-in as provided in para 3.1.2, or where boarding is denied on grounds of health, safety or
security, or for inadequate travel documentation, or where the passenger is accommodated in the same flight in a
lower class (in which case para 3.5 applies).

3.3 Cancellation

3.3.1 In case of cancellation:
(a) The airline shall inform passengers at least two weeks before the booked scheduled departure time and arrange
an alternate flight or refund the ticket, as acceptable to the passenger; or
(b) The airline shall inform passengers less than two weeks before and up to 24 hours before the booked scheduled
departure time and offer an alternate flight departing within two hours of the booked scheduled departure time, or
refund the ticket, as acceptable to the passenger.
No compensation is payable where the airline has complied with sub-para (a) or (b).

3.3.2 In case the airline fails to inform passengers as per para 3.3.1, or informs them less than 24 hours before the
booked scheduled departure time, or the passenger is informed of the cancellation on arrival at the airport, the
airline shall provide either an alternate flight or compensation, in addition to a full refund of the air ticket, as
given below:
(a) INR 5,000 or the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less, for flights having a
block time of up to and including one hour;
(b) INR 7,500 or the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less, for flights having a
block time of more than one hour and up to and including two hours;
(c) INR 10,000 or the booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less, for flights having a
block time of more than two hours.
Where the passenger accepts the alternate flight, no compensation under sub-paras (a) to (c) is payable. In all
cases the airline shall provide facilities in accordance with para 3.4.1 to passengers awaiting the alternate
flight.

3.3.3 The obligation to inform passengers under para 3.3.1 rests with the airline. Where the passenger did not
provide contact details (mobile number or e-mail address) at the time of booking, and the airline was for that
reason unable to inform the passenger, the airline shall not be liable to pay compensation under para 3.3.2. The
refund of the ticket remains payable.

3.3.4 The airline shall not be liable to pay compensation under para 3.3.2 if the cancellation is caused by
extraordinary circumstances as described in para 3.6. The refund of the ticket and the facilities under para 3.4
remain payable.

3.4 Delays

3.4.1 In case of delay, the airline shall provide meals and refreshments, free of charge, in relation to the
waiting time, when the departure is delayed (or the passenger awaits an alternate flight) by:
(a) 2 hours or more, for flights having a block time of up to and including 2 hours 30 minutes;
(b) 3 hours or more, for flights having a block time of more than 2 hours 30 minutes and up to and including
5 hours;
(c) 4 hours or more, for flights having a block time of more than 5 hours.

3.4.2 Where the departure of a flight is expected to be delayed by more than 6 hours and the airline informs the
passenger at least 24 hours before the booked scheduled departure time, the airline shall offer an alternate flight
departing within 6 hours of the booked scheduled departure time, or a full refund of the ticket, as acceptable to
the passenger.

3.4.3 Where the delay is more than 24 hours after the booked scheduled departure time, or where the booked
scheduled departure time is between 2000 hours and 0300 hours and the delay is more than 6 hours, the airline shall
provide hotel accommodation, including transfer, free of charge.

3.4.4 No monetary compensation is payable for delay under this CAR. The facilities under paras 3.4.1 to 3.4.3 are
payable irrespective of the cause of the delay, except that they are not required where providing them would itself
further delay the flight.

3.5 Downgrading

3.5.1 Where a passenger is downgraded to a class lower than that for which the ticket was purchased, the airline
shall, within seven days, refund:
(a) for domestic sectors, 75% of the cost of the ticket for that sector, including taxes;
(b) for international sectors of 1,500 km or less, 30% of the cost of the ticket for that sector, including taxes;
(c) for international sectors of more than 1,500 km and up to and including 3,500 km, 50% of the cost of the ticket
for that sector, including taxes;
(d) for international sectors of more than 3,500 km, 75% of the cost of the ticket for that sector, including taxes.

3.5.2 Para 3.5.1 does not apply where the downgrade is requested by the passenger.

3.6 Extraordinary circumstances

3.6.1 The airline shall not be required to pay compensation under paras 3.2.2 or 3.3.2 where the denied boarding or
cancellation is caused by extraordinary circumstances beyond the control of the airline, which could not have been
avoided even if all reasonable measures had been taken. Such circumstances include, but are not limited to,
political instability, natural disaster, civil war, insurrection or riot, flood, explosion, government regulation
or order, sabotage, strikes affecting the operations of the airline, security risks, weather conditions
incompatible with the operation of the flight, air traffic management decisions, and unexpected flight safety
shortcomings.

3.6.2 The burden of establishing extraordinary circumstances rests with the airline. Circumstances not listed in
para 3.6.1 shall be assessed against the general test in that para on the facts of each case.

3.7 Information to passengers

3.7.1 The airline shall inform passengers of their rights under this CAR, at check-in and on its website, and shall
display the passenger charter at its airport counters.

3.7.2 The airline shall obtain the contact details of passengers at the time of booking, including bookings made
through travel agents, for the purpose of informing them of cancellation or delay.

3.8 Grievance redressal

3.8.1 Every airline shall appoint a nodal officer to handle passenger grievances. A passenger who is not satisfied
with the airline's response may escalate the grievance through the AirSewa portal of the Ministry of Civil Aviation.
Where a claim is declined, the airline shall state the provision of this CAR under which it was declined.

3.9 Refunds

3.9.1 Refunds due under this CAR shall be processed within seven days for payments by credit card, immediately for
cash payments made at airline offices, and within thirty working days for tickets purchased through travel agents.
