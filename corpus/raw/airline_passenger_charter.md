SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT NOTICE. "Demo Air" is a fictional scheduled Indian carrier. This charter was written for the
project so that the system has a second document to reconcile with the CAR. Replace it with the passenger charter
of the airline your team selected (for example, from that airline's website) and re-run `make ingest`.

1. ABOUT THIS CHARTER

1.1 This charter sets out what Demo Air passengers can expect when their journey is disrupted. Where this charter
and the DGCA Civil Aviation Requirement on facilities to passengers (CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV) differ, the
passenger is entitled to whichever is more favourable, and our customer relations team will review the case.

2. CONTACT DETAILS

2.1 Please give us a working mobile number and e-mail address when you book, including when you book through a
travel agent. We use these to tell you about schedule changes. If we cannot reach you because no contact details
were provided, we may be unable to notify you of a cancellation in time.

3. CANCELLATIONS

3.1 If we cancel your flight we will offer you a seat on our next available flight to the same destination at no
extra charge, or a full refund, as you prefer.

3.2 If you are informed of a cancellation less than 24 hours before departure, or at the airport, compensation is
payable in line with the DGCA CAR unless you accept an alternate flight. Meal vouchers are provided while you wait
for the alternate flight.

4. DELAYS

4.1 For long delays we provide meals and refreshments at the airport in line with the DGCA CAR, and keep you
informed by SMS and e-mail every 30 minutes.

4.2 Hotel accommodation is provided where a delay exceeds 24 hours, or exceeds 6 hours for a flight scheduled to
depart between 2000 and 0300 hours.

5. DENIED BOARDING

5.1 If a flight is overbooked we will first ask for volunteers. Volunteers receive a travel voucher of INR 3,000 and
a seat on the next available flight.

5.2 Passengers must report at the boarding gate at least 25 minutes before departure. Passengers who report after
the boarding gate closes are treated as not having presented for boarding.

6. DOWNGRADING

6.1 Domestic sectors: if we seat you in a lower class than you paid for, we refund 75% of the ticket cost for that
sector, including taxes, within seven days.

6.2 International sectors: if we seat you in a lower class than you paid for, we refund the difference between the
fare paid and the fare of the class in which you travelled.

7. REFUNDS AND CLAIMS

7.1 Refunds are processed within seven days for card payments and within thirty working days for travel-agent
bookings.

7.2 Claims may be submitted through our website within 30 days of the travel date. If you are not satisfied with our
decision you may escalate through the AirSewa portal.
